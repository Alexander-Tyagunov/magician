# Research rubric

Grade the findings against each claim before the Completion Signal. Mark each claim met or unmet. List every unmet claim in the delivery, after the conclusion.

1. The findings restate the research question in one sentence at the top.
2. Every factual claim has a source: a URL, a library doc reference or a `file:line`.
3. The findings use at least the number of sources the chosen depth asks for (Quick overview 2, Standard depth 4, Deep dive 6), or say why fewer exist.
4. Every source has a date or a version. A claim from a source older than 18 months on a fast-moving topic carries a "may be out of date" note.
5. Contradictions between sources are listed with both sides. They are not dropped.
6. Fact and recommendation are in separate sections.
7. The conclusion answers the question asked, or names the evidence it lacks.
8. No query, URL or quote carries a private name, a secret or proprietary detail.
9. A finding that describes a system or a flow has a Mermaid block.
